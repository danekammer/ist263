Dane Kammer's Biography

Personal info

My name is Dane Kammer, and I am a student at Syracuse University, studying Innovation, Society, and Technology in the School of Information Studies. I am interested in how technology can be used to solve real-world problems and create new opportunities for people, and I particularly enjoy learning about emerging technologies such as artificial intelligence and how they can be applied across industries.

My skills and achievements include experience with Excel, leadership, teamwork, and communication. I have gained leadership experience through volunteer work and by working with children. The experience that meant the most to me was spending a month in Etinan, Nigeria, where I taught English and basic science to elementary-aged students. As a result of that experience, I learned to adapt, communicate with people from different backgrounds, and make the most of limited resources.

Apart from school and work, I enjoy skiing, spending time with my friends and family, and traveling. I also enjoy trying new things and visiting new places.
